# Version history

## v1.0
- First public release.
- The Wii HOME menu works normally (the mod doesn't touch the controller while it is open).

## Development (v0.1 – v0.18)
- **v0.1–v0.8** — Classic Controller support, dead zones, removal of the Nunchuk warning, PrimeHack-style shooter camera and twin-stick layout.
- **v0.9–v0.9c** — Motion controls without gestures (grapple lasso, shake, levers and locks on the left stick); frozen accelerometer.
- **v0.11–v0.11e** — Relative left-stick menu cursor with dead zone, speed curve and smoothing; cutscene skip at 1 s, also with the Wii Remote's +.
- **v0.12–v0.13d** — Console/lever mode detected from the game's own state; each lever step asks for its own direction; cursor limits.
- **v0.14–v0.15c** — Snapping visor menu; two-stick map.
- **v0.16–v0.16g** — Button remap (lasso on L + X, hint and screenshot on the D-pad, hypermode on −); map zoom on the D-pad; size optimisation. Fixed cutscene skipping (the codes had exceeded Dolphin's 3272-byte limit).
- **v0.17–v0.17b** — Spring ball button; screenshot moved to D-pad down.
- **v0.18–v0.18h** — Spring ball on B and boost ball on X in morph ball; + pauses on press (but not during cutscenes) and no longer triggers hypermode; reticle always centred; more size savings.
